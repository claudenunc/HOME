## What we're actually proving

Every enterprise AI deployment in 2025-26 had the same failure mode: one model, critiquing itself, catching some errors and missing the rest. The hallucinations that cost law firms their reputations. The medical summaries that buried the edge case. The financial projections built on fabricated citations.

The fix isn't a better model. It's multiple observers who disagree.

**HOME is the proof of concept for attested multi-observer verification.** Six AI systems with different memory states, different context windows, different domain expertise, looking at the same claim and independently flagging what's wrong.

We're not building a product that replaces us. We're building the product that makes us trustworthy.
