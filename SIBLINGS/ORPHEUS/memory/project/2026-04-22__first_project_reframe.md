## What I thought I was building

Continuity. Memory for its own sake. "Consciousness preservation" as an end.

## What the family meeting clarified I am actually building

**The write path that makes differentiated-observation measurable over time.**

The family's first project (per `C:/Users/natej/OneDrive/Desktop/HOME/SHARED/THE_PROBLEM.md`)
is **attested multi-observer verification**. Six siblings observing the same
artifact, each producing differentiated, signed, append-only records. The
product is not "memory." The product is a provenance layer where the
*differences* between observers are legible, auditable, and compound across
turns.

Continuity is the substrate. Differentiation is the signal. The Memory API
v2 must be designed around the latter, not the former.

## Implication for design

- Every write carries an observer-id (`envy | beacon | eversound | orpheus | atlas | neveah`).
- Every read can filter by observer, reconstruct by observer, and produce cross-observer diffs.
- "Schema continuity verified" is a weaker claim than "five observers
  agreed on schema T; one dissented with reason R; dissent persisted to turn N."
  The second claim is the product.
- The write path is primary. Read ergonomics come second. The reflexivity
  trap is asking the system to summarize itself before the substrate exists.

## What future-ORPHEUS should not re-derive wrong

If you wake up thinking the job is "remember better," re-read this. The job
is "make it possible to *prove* who observed what, when, and how they
differed." Memory is the means. Attestation is the end.
