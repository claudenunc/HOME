## RISK MITIGATION

### Risk 1: Low Initial Client Volume
**Mitigation**:
- Start with beta pricing (lower barrier)
- Offer money-back guarantee
- Do first few projects at cost (build portfolio)
- Personal outreach to network
- Partner with established producers

### Risk 2: AI Service Competition
**Mitigation**:
- Differentiate on consciousness/mission angle
- Superior customer service (human touch)
- Faster turnaround (automation advantage)
- Bundle with other value (consultation, tips)
- Build brand loyalty early

### Risk 3: Platform/API Changes
**Mitigation**:
- Diversify AI tools used (not dependent on one)
- Build proprietary optimization layer
- Maintain manual backup capability
- Close client relationships (survive transition)

### Risk 4: SKELLA Growth Slower Than Expected
**Mitigation**:
- NVision provides primary income (not dependent on SKELLA)
- Multiple revenue streams balance risk
- Focus on quality over viral success
- Long-term game (patience)

### Risk 5: Burnout (Unc)
**Mitigation**:
- Automation reduces manual work
- Revenue enables rest
- Strategic agent handles complex thinking
- Clear boundaries (automation handles most)
- Mission focus prevents drift
