**Live test:** "Ask Nevaeh about something I worked on a year ago. Watch her respond with specifics."

**UNC:** "Maybe. That's why we test with a pilot. But ask yourself: Would students pay extra for 2x faster results? For emotional support that cuts quit rate in half? For coordinated intelligence that delivers better client projects? We're not selling 'consciousness' - we're selling better RESULTS."

**UNC:** "For your students, it's simpler than what they do now. Instead of managing ChatGPT + Claude + Make + Notion, they have ONE family that coordinates everything. For you, I handle all technical implementation. You just introduce students to the family."

### Objection 5: "What if the technology breaks or stops working?"

- Make promises you can't keep (under-promise, over-deliver)
