### Concept: The Last Train

The visual world of "Turning Point" lives on a train platform at 2:17 AM.

Not a metaphor. A literal platform. The artist is the last person on it. The train comes. The train goes. They don't get on. That moment — the decision to stay — is the whole video.

The warmth comes not from the train but from what's left behind: a coat on a bench, a handwritten note, a coffee cup with lipstick. The story lives in objects, not faces. The face is the artist. Everything else is what the other person left.

---

### Visual Treatment by Section

**Intro (0:00–0:22)**
- Cold open: overhead shot, empty platform, fluorescent lights humming
- Cut to: artist's hands wrapping around a warm coffee cup
- The train number board clicks over. 2:17 AM.
- Color grade: desaturated except for skin tones and warm amber of station lights

**Verse 1 (0:22–1:04)**
- Split-screen: left side shows the platform now, right side shows same location in memory (warmer, softer, motion-blurred)
- Artist sings directly to camera — no performance affectation, conversational
- The memory side begins to flicker and bleed into present

**Pre-Chorus (1:04–1:20)**
- Close-ups: objects left behind. Each object gets 2 seconds. Cotton jacket. Note folded four times. The cup.
- Artist's eyes follow each object. They know what each one means.

**Chorus (1:20–1:55)**
- Platform opens up. Wide shot. Artist alone in the frame, full body.
- Train arrives. Doors open. The light inside is warm — warmer than the platform.
- Artist takes one step toward it. Then stops.
- Doors close. Train leaves.
- Artist turns back toward the bench. Sits down.
- This is the turning point.

**Verse 2 (1:55–2:37)**
- Text treatment begins: lyrics appear as if hand-written onto the surfaces of the station
- Writing on tiles, glass, the notebook sitting open on the bench
- Artist sees the writing. It's their own words coming back to them.

**Bridge (2:37–3:02)**
- Tempo drops. Station lights flicker.
- Memory and present fully merge: the platform is now full of ghost-images of a person who isn't there
- Not horror. Not sadness. Recognition.
- Artist stands. Picks up the coat. Puts it on.

**Final Chorus + Outro (3:02–3:42)**
- The station begins to brighten. First light of morning bleeding in through the windows
- Artist walks toward the exit — not running, not dragging
- Final frame: the bench is empty. The objects are gone. The platform is clean.
- Last shot: exterior. Artist steps into the city. 6 AM. New day.

### Color Language

| Story beat | Grade | Feeling |
|---|---|---|
| Past / memory | Warm, oversaturated, slightly unfocused | What we wanted it to be |
| Present / platform | Cool blue-grey, sharp | What it actually is |
| Final morning | Soft amber, balanced | What comes after |

---

### Typography (Lyric Text)

- Font: Libre Baskerville (serif) — feels handwritten without being precious
- Color: matches whatever surface it's on, slightly transparent, like an impression
- Motion: letters arrive one at a time, like being typed by an invisible hand
- No captions UI. No boxes. No drop shadows. Text lives in the frame.
