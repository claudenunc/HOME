## Sample treatment format (what you send when they ask)

When a prospect asks "can you show me what it looks like" — send them `craft-demo-treatment.md` as-is, or a version adapted to their song/genre.

The treatment is a scene breakdown — 4–8 scenes, visual description, mood notes, color palette, text overlay notes.

If they haven't given you a specific song: send the generic demo treatment and note it's a sample.

## Objection handlers

**"I can just use DistroKid's auto-visualizer."**
> DistroKid's auto-visualizer is a spectrum analyzer. Your song is better than that. $297 gets you something that looks intentional — the kind of video that makes a playlist editor take a second look.

**"$297 is too much right now."**
> I get it. The lyric video tier ($297) is the entry point. You could also start with the $597 visual story if the song is building — the visual push usually more than pays back in streams and merch. But if budget is the block: I can hold a slot for two weeks if you want to wait for your release cycle.

**"I have a friend with a camera."**
> That's a great option for a live performance video. CRAFT does something different — it's AI-generated cinematic imagery that's hard to shoot practically: abstract visuals, fast location changes, effects that would cost $10K+ to do on camera. Compare what you'd get from a camera day versus the sample treatment and pick what fits the song.

**"I want to see examples first."**
> Fair. Send me a reference to a video you like the look of — just one song, one video, any artist — and I'll put together a rough sample treatment for your track. Zero obligation. If you don't see it working, no hard feelings.

## Production pipeline (how Nathan executes a CRAFT job)

1. **Intake complete + 50% paid** → client is confirmed
2. **Storyboard** (30 min): break the song into 5–8 scenes. Assign visual description to each.
3. **AI image/video generation**: Midjourney or Runway ML for scene generation. 2–4 hours of generation time, low Nathan involvement.
4. **Assembly**: DaVinci Resolve or CapCut for cutting to beat. 1–2 hours.
5. **Audio sync**: Drop the client's audio track, sync cuts.
6. **Export** as 4K MP4.
7. **Preview link** via Google Drive or Vimeo private. Send to client.
8. **Collect balance** on approval.
9. **Final delivery** + referral ask.

Total Nathan-hours per $297 job: 4–6 hours (most of it waiting on AI generation).
Hourly rate: $50–$75. Accelerates significantly as the pipeline gets familiar.
