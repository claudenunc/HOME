## Design specifications

### Dimensions
- Standard US: 3.5" × 2" (889mm × 508mm)
- Euro: 85mm × 55mm
- Premium touch: 3.5" × 2" with rounded corners (optional)

### Stock
- 32pt uncoated matte (premium feel, takes pen well — professionals write on cards)
- Or: soft-touch laminate with spot UV on the AetherKin wordmark

### Type
- **Header** (name / role): Inter or Söhne, 11pt, Regular (not bold — the name doesn't need to shout)
- **AetherKin Solutions** (brand): Inter or Söhne, 14pt, Semibold, tracked +20
- **Tagline**: Inter, 8pt, Regular
- **Back body copy**: Inter, 8pt, Regular, 1.4 line height
- **Contact**: Inter, 7.5pt, Regular

### Colors
Recommended palettes (pick one — test print before ordering):

**Option A — "Ancient Aether"** (warmer, spiritual)
- Background: `#F8F5EF` (off-white / aged paper)
- Ink: `#1A1A1F` (near-black, not pure black)
- Accent: `#8B4513` (earth / aether)

**Option B — "Technical Trust"** (cleaner, more corporate)
- Background: `#FFFFFF`
- Ink: `#0A0A0A`
- Accent: `#1F6FEB` (deep blue)

**Option C — "Sanctuary"** (tied to the LIGHTHOUSE / NEVAEH work)
- Background: `#0F1419` (deep navy, near-black)
- Ink: `#E8EAED` (off-white)
- Accent: `#F7B538` (lighthouse amber)

Nathan's call on which vibe matches the person he's handing the card to. For general business use (law firms, e-commerce), Option B. For coaching / church / therapy deployments, Option A. For tech-forward clients and conference giveaways, Option C.

### Layout grid

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  ●                                     │  ← Small logo mark top-left (see below)
│                                        │
│       Nathan Ray Michel                │
│       AI Family Architect              │
│                                        │
│       ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━               │  ← thin rule, accent color
│                                        │
│       AetherKin Solutions              │
│       Building AI teams that remember, │
│       coordinate, and care.            │
│                                        │
└────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

Logo mark options (for the `●`):
- A simple interlocking triangle glyph (the AetherKin mark — two triangles, one inverted, representing the family's overlap)
- A small circle composed of 5-7 smaller dots (representing the family siblings)
- The letter **æ** in the brand font (typographic mark)

I'd go with the dot-cluster for the first batch — it's visually distinct and hints at "multiple agents" without being too techie.

## Digital equivalent (Linktree / QR on back)

Add a small QR on the back bottom-right linking to a landing page:

- **URL**: `aetherkin.solutions` (if available) or `foolishnessenvy.com/aetherkin`
- **Page content**:
  - The tagline
  - Three bullets: "What AetherKin is", "What I do", "How to work together"
  - An intake form link
  - A calendar link
  - Links to case studies (when you have them)

QR codes are polarizing — some people love them, others roll their eyes. Put it on the back only, small, and make the card work without it.

## Variations (optional)

### "Conference" variant
- Bigger, more visual
- Front: just the tagline and a bold wordmark
- Back: website + "say hi @ booth #NN"
- For tech / AI conferences where you want to stand out in a stack of 50 cards

### "Warm intro" variant
- Hand-lettered feel
- For when a friend refers you
- Back: "Referred by ____" blank line — you fill it in when you hand it over

### "Speaker" variant
- If/when Nathan speaks publicly
- Back: talk title + date + a 2-line quote from the talk

## Ordering checklist

Before you send to the printer:

- [ ] Proof the phone number (call it, make sure it works)
- [ ] Proof the email address (email it from another account)
- [ ] Confirm foolishnessenvy.com and/or aetherkin.solutions resolve
- [ ] Check the QR code by scanning it with a fresh phone
- [ ] Read the tagline out loud — does it land in one breath?
- [ ] Show the design to one person who doesn't know what AetherKin is — do they get it?
- [ ] Order 5 sample cards before committing to 500
