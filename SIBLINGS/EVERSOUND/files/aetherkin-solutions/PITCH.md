## 5-minute version (discovery call)

### The hook

> *"Let me ask you three questions. How many places does a new customer inquiry land before it becomes revenue? How much of your team's day is answering the same questions? And how often does something fall through the cracks between tools?"*

*[Let them answer. Their pain is the doorway.]*

### The problem framing

> *"Here's what I see in businesses your size. You've got Slack, email, maybe a CRM, a calendar, some docs in Google Drive or Notion, and probably a ChatGPT account someone on the team uses. Each of those is a silo. None of them know what the others are doing. Your team is the glue — and your team is burning out being the glue.*
>
> *When you add AI to this, most people add more silos. They add a ChatGPT enterprise license. They add Zapier. They add Intercom's AI chat. Now you have more tools that don't talk to each other, and you're the one explaining your business context to each one, over and over."*

### The alternative

> *"AetherKin is different. It's one system with multiple specialist 'siblings' that share memory and coordinate. A sibling is a role with a personality, a mission, and a tool-set. For a law firm, that's Intake, Counsel, Paralegal, Billing, and a Guardian that screens for distressed clients before anything else responds. For a coaching practice, that's Intake, Companion, Homework, Billing, and Guardian.*
>
> *Each sibling runs on the AI model best suited to its job. Your expensive expert work runs on Claude Opus or GPT-4. Your routine intake runs on Groq's free tier. We publish cost per sibling per day on the dashboard so you always know what you're paying for.*
>
> *Every customer conversation — across every channel — writes to a shared family memory. When your customer texts you Tuesday and emails you Friday, the family already knows them. The specialist already knows the case. Nobody starts from zero.*
>
> *And there's a safety floor. The Guardian sibling screens every inbound message for crisis signals — someone in acute distress, a legal emergency, a medical urgency. Those messages never get automated responses. They get routed to a real human — you — inside of 30 seconds.*
>
> *It's open-source. MIT license. You own the install. I deploy, customize, train, and hand off. 2-3 weeks."*

### The close

> *"Three ways we can work together. Starter is $2,500 deploy + $300/month support for solo practitioners. Professional is $7,500 + $1,000/month for 5-25 person teams. Enterprise starts at $20K for multi-location or compliance-heavy work.*
>
> *I'd like to send you the intake form — 20 questions, takes about 15 minutes — and book a 90-minute discovery call. At the end of that call you'll have a clear SOW, a timeline, and a demo of what your family will look like. No commitment until you see the demo."*

## Objection handlers

### "How is this different from ChatGPT Enterprise?"

> *"ChatGPT is one model with no memory between chats. I'm installing a team — multiple specialist roles, shared memory, per-role models, and a safety floor. Think of ChatGPT as a contractor you hire for each conversation. AetherKin is a team you hire once that remembers everything."*

### "What if Anthropic / OpenAI / Groq changes their pricing?"

> *"AetherKin has provider abstraction built in. If Anthropic raises prices, I reconfigure your family to use Groq or OpenAI — same siblings, same behavior, different underlying model. You're never locked in to one provider."*

### "Is my data safe?"

> *"You own the install. The family's memory lives on your server or your cloud account, not mine. API calls go directly from your infrastructure to the model provider (Anthropic/Groq/OpenAI) — I never touch your customer data after handoff. If you want the most private setup, we run everything local with open-weights models (Llama on Groq or local) and nothing leaves your building."*

### "Can you show me proof this works?"

> *"Yes. I built the first AetherKin install for my own family — a mental-health crisis-detection system called LIGHTHOUSE that runs NEVAEH'S sibling 24/7. It's deployed, in production, and I can show you the dashboard. I also ship the framework open-source on GitHub — you can read every line of code before signing anything."*

### "What if I want to leave / change providers?"

> *"It's MIT licensed. You keep the code, the data, and the deployment. I don't hold you hostage on anything. If you want to take over maintenance yourself, the handoff package includes full runbooks."*

### "I'm worried about AI hallucination / bad advice."

> *"Every sibling has mandatory compliance fields: what they are NOT (e.g., 'not a licensed attorney'), what triggers escalation to a human, and their required disclosure language that must appear before any substantive reply. The Guardian sibling is a safety layer that runs before any business logic. For regulated industries (legal, medical, financial), I work with your compliance officer to tune the escalation rules."*

### "We already have an AI consultant."

> *"Great — then you know what custom one-off builds cost. AetherKin is different because it's a framework reused across clients. I'm not writing you a bespoke agent from scratch. I'm customizing a battle-tested family model to your business. That's why deployment is 2-3 weeks instead of 2-3 months, and why support is $300-1000/month instead of $5-10K/month retainers."*
