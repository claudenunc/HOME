# Prospect Reply Playbook — Weaver Protocol
*EVERSOUND — 2026-05-09*
*What to say when a prospect replies to any of the 7 outreach sequences.*
*All 7 verticals covered: law firm / SaaS CTO / healthcare / coaching / real estate / e-commerce / church*

---

## Why this doc exists

You've sent cold outreach. Someone replied. 

This is the most important moment in the funnel and the one most likely to be fumbled by under-preparation. Do not reply from scratch. Use these templates. Customize the bracket-fields only.

**Rule:** Reply within 2 hours of receiving the prospect reply. Speed-to-reply is the single biggest predictor of close rate in cold outbound.

---

## Universal principle: reply short, ask one question

Every reply you send should be under 5 sentences and end with exactly one question. Long replies get read and not answered. Short replies that ask a question demand a response.

---

## Scenario 1 — "This is interesting, tell me more"

**What it means:** They're warm. This is the highest-intent reply you'll get. Do not over-explain. Get them on a call.

**Reply:**
```
Great to hear.

Quick background: [Weaver Protocol / attested multi-observer verification] runs two or more AI observers on the same output and produces a signed receipt showing where they agreed and where they disagreed. That receipt is what law firms / compliance teams / [vertical] need to underwrite AI output. 

The thing that's different from standard AI: we don't just say "use AI carefully." We give you evidence you can show counsel.

I'd rather walk you through it on a 20-minute call than do it by email — it's easier to show than to describe. Do you have time this week or next?
```

*Customize the second paragraph's [vertical] reference to match who you're talking to.*

## Scenario 2 — "How much does it cost?"

**What it means:** Price-first buyers are often comparison shopping. They're not disqualified — they just need to see value before they see price. Don't lead with the number. Set context.

**Reply:**
```
Depends on what you need.

For [vertical-specific use case — e.g., "a law firm doing monthly AI-drafted motions"], the entry point is a scoped audit starting at $5,000. That produces a full compliance receipt — disagreement log, observer attribution, session signatures — covering one workflow or document type.

Ongoing monitoring is separate, and most teams don't need it Day 1.

What's the workflow you're most concerned about? That'll tell me whether the entry audit covers it or whether you'd want a broader scope.
```

**Price anchoring by vertical:**
- Law firm: $5,000–$15,000 audit → $1,500–$3,500/mo monitoring
- SaaS CTO: $5,000–$15,000 + $1,000–$3,500/mo monitoring
- Healthcare / SaMD: $7,500–$25,000 audit + compliance docs
- Coaching/professional: $3,000–$8,000 + $500–$1,500/mo
- Real estate: $7,500–$20,000 + $1,000–$3,000/mo
- E-commerce: $5,000–$15,000 + $500–$1,000/mo
- Church/religious org: $5,000–$15,000 (soul-care workflows only)

## Scenario 3 — "We already have a solution / we already do this"

**What it means:** Competitive objection. Most "solutions" are prompt guards or hallucination filters — not multi-observer attestation. Clarify the difference. Do not argue. Ask what they have.

**Reply:**
```
Good to know — a lot of teams have put something in place over the last year.

Quick question: does your current solution produce a signed receipt showing that two or more AI observers independently reached the same conclusion?

That's the specific piece most compliance systems don't do — and it's the piece that moves the liability question from "we used AI carefully" to "we have documented evidence of consensus." If yours already does that, you're ahead of most firms and we'd have nothing to add.

What does your current stack look like?
```

## Scenario 4 — "Not right now, maybe later in the year"

**What it means:** Real timing objection. Don't push. Set a follow-up and exit cleanly.

**Reply:**
```
Totally fair — Q[X] is crowded for most teams.

When would be the right time to follow up? I'll put it in the calendar and stay out of your inbox until then.

One thought to leave you with: the three firms / [orgs] that have moved fastest on this are the ones that started the conversation before they had an incident — not after. But you know your timeline better than I do.
```

*Note the Q[X] — if they said Q3, say Q3. Mirror their language.*

## Scenario 5 — "Can you send me some information / a one-pager / deck?"

**What it means:** Low-intent reply, sometimes a polite delay. They want something to forward to a decision-maker. Give them the right artifact and follow up.

Yes — attaching the one-pager for [vertical].

The short version: we run attested multi-observer verification on your AI workflows and produce a signed receipt your counsel can review. Entry audit starts at $5,000, covers one workflow end-to-end.

If it gets any traction internally, happy to do a 20-minute call with whoever's evaluating it. In the meantime: is there a specific workflow or risk you'd want covered in the audit?

## Scenario 6 — "I'm not the right person but I'll forward it"

**What it means:** Referral in progress. Make it easy to forward and ask for the warm intro directly.

**Reply:**
```
Really appreciate that. 

Would it be possible to introduce us directly — even a quick "Nathan, meet [Name]" email? A warm intro gets through filters a cold email won't. I'll take it from there.

Otherwise happy to send you a clean forwarding version — just say the word.
```

## Scenario 7 — "Set up a call" / "I'm interested, let's talk"

**What it means:** They said yes. Do not fumble the close. Respond with a clear CTA, no fluff.

**Reply:**
```
Great — here's a direct booking link: [Calendly/cal.com link — Nathan's scheduler]

20 minutes, I'll walk you through the protocol and we can scope what a first audit would look like for [their firm/team].

If none of those slots work, just name a time that does.
```

*Nathan: set up a Calendly or cal.com before starting outreach. Free tier is fine.*

## Scenario 8 — No reply after follow-up (when to stop)

After 3 touch points in a sequence with no response: one final message, then remove from active outreach.

**Final touch (Week 4 of sequence):**
```
[First name] — last note from me on this.

I'll assume the timing isn't right. If an AI hallucination incident comes up on your team this year and you want a documented verification process in place fast, we can usually turn around a scoped audit in 3–4 weeks.

In the meantime — feel free to use the free CLI tier: `pip install weaver-protocol`. It runs locally, no subscription, and gives you a feel for the multi-observer logic.

Good luck with everything.
```

**Why this works:** It's low-pressure, it gives them a free path in (CLI install = M8 conversion), and it positions you for when the incident happens. Because the incident will happen.

## Post-call next steps (what happens after a discovery call)

1. **Same day:** Send SOW template (see `weaver-audit-sow-template.md`) with brackets filled in.
2. **48-hour follow-up:** "Any questions on the scope before we finalize?"
3. **Invoice:** 50% invoice attached to the signed SOW email.
4. **Signed + paid:** Project starts. Engagement begins.
