## The problem in 60 seconds

A real estate brokerage deploys an AI assistant to help agents write property listings, answer buyer questions, and research comparable sales. The AI generates a listing that describes a neighborhood's demographics in terms that trigger Fair Housing Act violations. The brokerage did not catch it before it went live. The DOJ investigates.

This is not a hypothetical edge case. The Fair Housing Act (42 U.S.C. § 3604) prohibits discriminatory representations in real estate — and AI models trained on biased data reproduce those patterns. When an AI output is used in a real estate transaction without verification, the liability attaches to the brokerage, not the model.

**The gap:** real estate firms are using AI to accelerate listing copy, buyer communications, market analysis, and contract summaries — without any verification layer. One discriminatory output goes live, and the firm has a federal civil rights exposure they cannot explain to their E&O insurer.

## What Weaver Protocol does

Weaver Protocol runs multiple AI observers on the same input, compares outputs, flags disagreements, and generates a signed attestation report. For real estate, this means:

## The compliance landscape

**Fair Housing Act (federal):** Prohibits discriminatory language, steering, and misrepresentation in real estate. AI-generated content is not exempt. DOJ and HUD have both issued guidance that automated systems used in housing decisions must comply.

**RESPA (Real Estate Settlement Procedures Act):** AI-assisted loan referrals or settlement service recommendations carry kickback and disclosure obligations. AI that misrepresents fees or relationships creates RESPA exposure.

**NAR Code of Ethics (Article 12):** Requires truthful representation. AI-generated listing copy that overstates property features or misrepresents condition violates Article 12 — and licensing boards are beginning to hold brokerages accountable for AI outputs under their license.

**E&O Insurance:** Errors and Omissions insurers are adding AI exclusions or surcharges for brokerages without documented AI governance. A Weaver attestation report is the documented governance policy.

**California SB 1047 / State AI Disclosure Laws (2025-26):** Multiple states now require disclosure of AI-generated content in consumer-facing real estate materials. Weaver's attestation log provides the disclosure record.
