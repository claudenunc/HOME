## Step 2: How to Engage (NOT How to Pitch)

The rule: **give value first, identify yourself second, never pitch cold on social.**

**Good reply (adds to the conversation):**
> "The multi-observer consensus gap is what we keep running into too — it's one thing to prove execution integrity, another to attest correctness. Have you seen approaches that close both in the same receipt?"

**Good reply (positions without selling):**
> "Layer 1 (did it run?) is getting solved fast. The harder problem is Layer 2 (was it right?). The malpractice carrier doesn't care about the cryptographic proof if the output was wrong."

**What NOT to do:**
- Don't drop "pip install weaver-protocol" in a reply thread cold
- Don't reply to 10 posts in a row with similar language — it reads as spam
- Don't argue with NexArt or other companies' approaches — be additive
