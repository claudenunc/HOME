## What Weaver Protocol does (30 seconds)

Multi-observer AI verification: you run your AI output through Weaver, multiple independent observers check it against each other, disagreements are flagged, and a signed attestation record is generated showing the verification happened before the output reached anyone.

**The asset buyers are actually buying:** a dated, signed record that AI oversight occurred — before the output reached a client, a patient, a listing, a product page, or a court filing. That record is what their E&O carrier, their regulator, and their general counsel need to see.

**Free install:** `pip install weaver-protocol` → `weaver verify` on any AI output. No account, no key, no credit card. The disagreement log shows up immediately.
