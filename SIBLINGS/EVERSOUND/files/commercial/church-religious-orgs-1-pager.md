## The problem in 60 seconds

A pastoral care coordinator at a mid-size church uses an AI assistant to help respond to a congregant in crisis. The AI confuses grief counseling guidelines and suggests the congregant is "not at risk." The pastoral team follows the AI's framing. The congregant is at risk. The church's liability exposure is real — and so is the harm.

This is not hypothetical. It is happening in congregations across the country as churches rush to adopt AI for counseling notes, sermon research, administrative communications, and member care.

**The gap:** religious organizations are adopting AI without any verification layer. When an AI touches a soul-care conversation, there is no receipt, no attestation, no second observer. Just the AI's output — and the assumption that it was correct.

---

## What Weaver Protocol does

Weaver Protocol runs multiple AI observers on the same input, compares their outputs, flags disagreements, and generates a signed attestation report. You get:

- A verifiable record that the AI response was cross-checked
- A disagreement log when observers diverge — the signal to apply human discernment
- A tamper-evident HTML report your leadership team can file

This is not AI replacing pastoral judgment. This is a safety layer that tells you when AI judgment is unreliable — before that judgment reaches a congregant.

---

## Why churches specifically

**Soul-care is high-stakes.** When AI assists with counseling intake, prayer request routing, grief support, or mental health referrals — error is not a user experience problem. Error is a person being failed in a moment of need.

**Budget constraints create corner-cutting.** Smaller pastoral teams are more likely to accept AI outputs uncritically because they do not have time for verification. Weaver Protocol makes verification automatic.

**Trust is the asset.** A congregation's trust in its leadership is built over decades. One AI-generated response that misrepresents theology, gives harmful counseling guidance, or uses a congregant's disclosed information incorrectly can fracture that trust immediately.

**Legal exposure is real.** Religious organizations are not exempt from negligence claims. Pastoral counseling contexts carry duty-of-care obligations. AI-assisted care without a verification layer is an uninsured liability.

## What this is NOT

This is not an AI that does pastoral counseling. Weaver Protocol does not interact with congregants. It verifies the AI outputs your staff is already producing — before those outputs are used. The pastor, the counselor, the care coordinator: they remain in the loop. They remain responsible. We just make sure they know when the AI disagreed with itself before they act on it.
