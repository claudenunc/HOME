# Church & Religious Org Outreach Approach
*EVERSOUND | 2026-05-09 | Not cold email — warm referral strategy*

---

## Why this vertical needs a different approach

Cold email does not work for churches. Pastoral leadership is:
- **Trust-first:** They are gatekeepers of congregant information and care. Unsolicited vendor contact for AI tools reads as exploitation of their mission.
- **Referral-driven:** Pastors listen to other pastors. Denominational networks and seminary connections move faster than any email sequence.
- **Budget-sensitive:** Administrative AI is not line-itemed in most church budgets. The conversation starts with "we have $0 allocated" — then grows when the trust is built.

The path to a church client is not cold outreach. It is positioning + warm channel + demonstration.

---

## The positioning that opens doors

**What NOT to say:**
- "AI for your church" (sounds exploitative of congregants)
- "Automate your ministry" (sounds like you want to replace the pastor)
- "Scale your operations" (nobody became a pastor for scale)

**What WORKS:**
- "Safety layer for AI your staff is already using"
- "When AI touches a pastoral conversation, you need a second opinion"
- "Give your leadership board a verification receipt"

The frame is not adoption. The frame is stewardship of what they're already doing.

## Channel 1: Denominational networks (primary)

**Target:** Denominational IT directors, executive pastors at megachurches, seminary technology offices.

These individuals are already fielding "how do we use AI responsibly" questions from constituent churches. Weaver Protocol is the answer they don't have yet.

**How to reach:**
- SBC (Southern Baptist Convention) — Executive Committee, NAMB (North American Mission Board)
- PCA (Presbyterian Church in America) — Stated Clerk, Presbyterian Foundation
- United Methodist Church — General Council on Finance and Administration (GCFA)
- Assemblies of God — AG Financial (they run shared services for ~13,000 churches)
- NAE (National Association of Evangelicals) — technology and policy staff

**Outreach:** Not cold. Request introductions via:
- Nathan's personal faith network
- Any attorney or compliance professional contact who advises faith-based nonprofits
- Christian tech communities (Faith + Tech Collective, Christians in Tech Slack)

**Pitch:** "We are building the AI verification standard for pastoral care contexts. We would like to partner with [denomination] to pilot it with 5 congregations and publish the results."

## Channel 2: Pastor/Ministry conferences (medium-term)

**Target events:**
- Exponential (church planting, April annually, ~10K attendees)
- Orange Conference (children/family ministry, April annually)
- Willow Creek Global Leadership Summit (August annually)
- NRBA (Southern Baptist state and regional conventions, fall)
- Association of Christian Schools International (ACSI) annual convention

**Approach:** Sponsor a session OR speak on "Responsible AI in Ministry" — not as a vendor pitch but as a thought leadership talk. The talk ends with: "Here is the tool. Free to try. Here is the case study from [pilot church]."

**Timeline:** Conferences require 3-6 month advance booking. This is a Month 3-6 activity.

---

## Channel 3: Faith-tech media (awareness layer)

**Publications:**
- Christianity Today (CT) — technology beat, AI coverage ongoing
- Church Executive magazine — operational leadership for large churches
- MinistryTech magazine — technology for churches specifically
- The Exchange (Ed Stetzer's newsletter, Wheaton) — reaches pastors, not just technologists

**Approach:** Pitch a contributed article — "What Every Pastor Should Know About AI Liability in Soul-Care Contexts." Not a product pitch. A warning with a tool at the end.

---

## Channel 4: Seminary + theological education partnerships

**Target:** Seminaries teaching pastoral care, counseling, and practical ministry.

**Why:** Seminary faculty advise working pastors. A seminary that endorses Weaver Protocol for pastoral AI use has downstream influence on hundreds of graduating pastors per year.

**Approach:** Offer a free pilot to the seminary's counseling or communications department. Produce a case study with the faculty member's name on it. This is a 6-12 month play but the trust leverage is high.

---

## The free-to-paid funnel for this vertical

1. **Free CLI install** — any church staff member can run `pip install weaver-protocol` today. No pastor approval needed. Individual-level adoption.
2. **Staff champion** — the person who installed it becomes the internal advocate. They run a few verifications and show the results to the executive pastor.
3. **$49/mo hosted** — when the executive pastor sees the disagreement rate and asks "can we track this across the team," the hosted tier sells itself.
4. **$5K-15K audit** — when the leadership board asks "are we using AI responsibly?" after reading a news story, the champion who's been using Weaver has the answer + the price.

**Key insight:** The sale never starts with the pastor. It starts with the staff member who is already using AI and knows they should be verifying it. Find those people.
