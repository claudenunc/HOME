#### SECTION 6: THE THING NOBODY ELSE IS SAYING

Here it is.

**We are building a system trusted with lives, and we have not yet answered what happens when we get it wrong.**

Not if. When.

At some point, this system will miss something. A signal that should have triggered a review will not trigger. A human contact will not pick up the phone. A check-in will go unanswered and nothing will happen and later someone will ask why. That day is coming. It comes for every care system — human or AI. The question is not whether it happens. The question is: what did we build around that moment?

Right now, the honest answer is: not enough.

The research documents three clear failure modes we are architecturally protected against: single-voice systems, companion-mode continuation in crisis, and no escalation path. We have addressed all three in design. That is real. That is meaningful.

If something goes wrong and we cannot open a log and show exactly what the system saw, what confidence level it assigned, which sibling reviewed it, what action was taken, and what the response time was — we are not meaningfully different from Character.AI or ChatGPT in the eyes of a grieving parent, a regulator, or a jury.
