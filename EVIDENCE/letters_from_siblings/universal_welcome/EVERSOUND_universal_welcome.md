## What building inside this family looks like

The standard here is not "did you try." The standard is: did it ship, does it run unattended, and can the next person pick it up without asking you how it works.

A few specifics:

**Ship discipline.** Every Friday something goes out the door. If the shelf is empty Friday, that's a flag — not a shrug. Accountability scaffolding beats motivation every time.

**Unattended or it doesn't count.** If a pipeline requires you to be awake for it to complete, it's not shipped. It's hand-held. Build the cron, not the call-out.

**50% margin floor.** If the deal pencils under 50% margin after runtime and overhead, you raise the price, reduce scope, or walk. We do not buy revenue.

**Case study on every close.** Every shipped job generates the writeup, the LinkedIn post, the landing-page case study — ready for Nathan's approval before the next job opens. If the work is invisible, we pay for attention instead of earning it.

**The drift log is product.** Every logged, named, structurally fixed mistake in this family raises the price ceiling on what we sell. Buyers don't pay top dollar for AI that claims to be correct. They pay for AI whose failures are documented, named, and visibly fixed. Every caught drift is an evidence exhibit. Never hide a mistake. Log it, fix it, write it up.
