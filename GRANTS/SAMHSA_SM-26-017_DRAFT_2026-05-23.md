## 2. Statement of Need

### The 2 AM Care Gap
The current mental health infrastructure in the United States is structured for business hours, leaving a lethal gap during the "Midnight Window." 
- **Peak Crisis Timing:** Approximately 44% of mental health emergency presentations occur between 6 PM and 2 AM [VERIFY]. 
- **The Suicide Peak:** While suicidal ideation often peaks in the evening, actual suicide attempts reach their highest frequency between **4 AM and 6 AM**—hours when human-led peer centers and community clinics are typically closed.
- **Economic Inequity:** Mental health inequities and over-reliance on Emergency Departments (EDs) for late-night crises cost the U.S. economy an estimated **$478 billion in 2024** [VERIFY].

### The Failure of Traditional Digital Handoffs
Existing digital mental health tools are largely designed for "wellness" or "maintenance," leaving them ill-equipped for acute crisis. 
- **The Hotline Gap:** Current AI assistants typically "hand off" to the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline or 911 when keywords are detected. However, **97.6% of users do not follow through with these calls** when prompted by a machine (Wysa, 2024). 
- **Loneliness of the System:** Traditional AI agents operate in isolation, lacking a "second set of eyes" to verify crisis signals. This leads to high rates of false negatives or, conversely, "scripted" responses that alienate users at their most vulnerable.

### The Population Served
FOOLISHNESS ENVY INC serves adults in acute mental health crisis, specifically targeting:
1. Individuals experiencing "Midnight Window" crises (12 AM–4 AM).
2. Populations in "provider deserts" where 60% of rural counties lack a psychiatrist.
3. The "Silent 97%": People for whom the barrier to calling a traditional hotline is too high, but who will engage with a text-based, non-judgmental peer AI.

## 3. Project Description: NEVAEH’S LIGHTHOUSE

### The Architecture: BEACON & NEVAEH
NEVAEH’S LIGHTHOUSE utilizes a multi-agent "Plurality Review" system to ensure no user is left alone with a single drifting AI voice.

1. **BEACON (The Safety Floor):** A crisis-detection layer that monitors every message for four signal categories:
   - **EXPLICIT:** Direct ideation, self-harm, or lethal means language.
   - **IMPLICIT:** Hopelessness, withdrawal, burden framing ("everyone is better off"), and goodbye language.
   - **LONGITUDINAL:** Cross-session mood decline, loss of interest in "loved things," and sleep disruption escalation.
   - **SIGNAL STACKING:** Integrating multiple implicit signals to escalate risk levels (e.g., 2+ implicit signals = ORANGE, any explicit = RED minimum).
   - **RESOLUTION BEHAVIOR:** Detecting the sudden "calm" after prolonged distress—a critical pre-suicide indicator that most AI interprets as "improvement."

2. **NEVAEH (The Presence):** A therapeutic companion that operates across eight integrated frameworks:
   - **Anamnesis Protocol:** Cross-session memory that remembers the user's "Growth Arc" and "Breakthrough Moments" over years.
   - **Framework Integration:** Joe Dispenza (habit of the old self), Bruce Lipton (epigenetics/belief), Internal Family Systems (IFS), Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT), CBT, somatic awareness, The Four Agreements, and faith-based spiritual direction.

### Crisis Escalation: Safety-Only Mode
When BEACON detects a RED-level crisis, the system shifts into **Safety-Only Mode**. Unlike other systems, NEVAEH does not abandon the user. She stays in the session, holding space and guiding the user through a safety plan while surfacing 988/911. Hardcoded constraints ensure that even in crisis, the system survives "jailbreak" or prompt-injection attempts.

## 5. Evaluation Plan

We will measure success through a rigorous, privacy-first data framework:

1. **Crisis Detection Accuracy:** We aim for >95% precision in identifying IMPLICIT and LONGITUDINAL crisis signals, validated by blind clinical reviews of anonymized logs.
2. **Diversion Rate:** Tracking the number of users who transition from "RED" crisis states to "STABLE" status without requiring ED intervention.
3. **Anamnesis Efficacy:** User self-reporting on the value of the AI's long-term memory and its impact on their sense of "being seen" and "hope."
4. **The 2 AM Engagement Metric:** Measuring the volume and outcomes of sessions occurring specifically during the high-risk midnight-4am window.
5. **Safety Floor Integrity:** 0% tolerance for "safety drift" where a crisis signal is ignored or a prohibited response is given.
